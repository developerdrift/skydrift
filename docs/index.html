How to spot a mover, in 3 steps
  1. Pick a spot. Tap a preset or type a position. SkyDrift collects every SPHEREx picture of it.
  2. Watch the blink. Each frame is one visit, months apart. Background stars stay put. A nearby star or brown dwarf hops from frame to frame.
  3. Check it. Switch to Subtract: a mover leaves a bright/dark pair. Look at the wavelength and dates in the panel before believing anything.

Why it's fair: SPHEREx sees each spot at a different wavelength in every exposure, so a naive blink flickers for no real reason. We stack each detector per visit, so only real changes show.
