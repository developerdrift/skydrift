Does the pipeline measure known stellar motions correctly?

← back to the blink tool

For known nearby high-proper-motion stars, SkyDrift measures how far each star moved between its first and last SPHEREx visit (same detector, after removing the field's median offset). It compares that with the shift predicted from the star's proper motion and parallax.

Data: SPHEREx (NASA/IPAC IRSA). Comparison values: literature (see caveats).
