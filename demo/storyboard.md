# SkyDrift: 30-second demo storyboard

**Rules:** 30 s maximum, English subtitles (`subtitles.srt`), screen recording of the real app
plus files from `demo/`. No AI-generated images, no NASA logos, insignia or patches.
Every shot is SPHEREx data rendered by SkyDrift. All assets are regenerated by
`scripts/refresh.py`, so use the latest ones.

| Time | Shot | Source | Subtitle |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:04 | App home page; tap the "WISE 0855-0714" preset; progress bar fills. | screen recording, `python -m app.server` or `docs/` | Pick any spot on the sky. SkyDrift gathers every SPHEREx picture of it. |
| 0:04-0:09 | Exposure blink for one visit: the field flickers as the wavelength changes; zoom on the label showing µm. | app, Mode = Exposure blink | SPHEREx sees each spot at a different wavelength every time, so a raw blink flickers. |
| 0:09-0:14 | Switch to Visit blink: the field is steady and one star hops. | app, Mode = Visit blink (or `demo/barnard_blink.mp4`) | Stacking per visit and per detector keeps only real changes. |
| 0:14-0:19 | WISE 0855 panel: invisible at 1 µm, glowing at 4.6 µm. | `demo/wise0855_D1_vs_D6.png` | The coldest known brown dwarf: invisible at 1 µm, bright at 4.6 µm. |
| 0:19-0:24 | Candidates list: "known target ... measured 4.1″ vs predicted 4.6″"; tap it and the marker zooms. | app, Candidates panel | SkyDrift finds movers itself and measures how far they went. |
| 0:24-0:30 | `/validation` page: measured vs predicted plot with the 1:1 line and the RMS number. | `docs/validation.html` | And it checks itself against known stars. Try it on any patch of sky. |

**Recording notes**
- Use the 2-second blink speed (slider at 3) so each frame is readable.
- Keep the crosshair on and the browser at about 1000 px wide.
- Read the numbers from the live app on recording day. The daemon keeps adding data until
  21:30, so the values in the subtitles may need a tweak.
- Don't call anything a discovery. The wording above only claims measurements.
